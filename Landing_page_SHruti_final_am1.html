About Me

I’m a Graphic & UX Designer who loves turning ideas into purposeful, intuitive, and visually engaging experiences. Raised with vibrant culture and creativity in Pune, Maharashtra, I grew up believing that every design should feel like a story waiting to unfold.

My journey has taken me through branding, UI/UX design, visual storytelling, social media design, international student recruitment, and community-focused creative projects. This mix of creative + operational experience helps me craft solutions that are insightful, functional, and emotionally expressive.

I’m proud to be recognized by the India Book of Records and Harvard World Records for creating the Largest Written Rangoli — a reminder that design for me isn’t just a skill, it’s a celebration of patience, precision, and artistic storytelling.

Currently, I’m pursuing my Master’s in UX Management & Design at PFH University – Göttingen, where I’m refining my ability to blend strategy, usability, and creative innovation into every design decision I make.

Whether I’m designing an interface, shaping a brand, illustrating concepts, or simplifying complex problems, my goal remains the same — to create meaningful, memorable, and human-centered experiences that leave people feeling understood, engaged, and delighted.
